Questions & answers

Do I need a credit card to start?
No — the Free plan requires no credit card. You connect your Google account and start using Aura immediately.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. Cancel from Settings → Billing at any time. You keep access until the end of the billing period and won't be charged again.
Does Aura read or store my emails?
Aura reads your emails only to generate summaries and triage classifications. Email content is never stored on our servers — we query Gmail in real time and pass content to the AI model ephemerally. No email data is retained after the request completes.
What email providers do you support?
Gmail and Google Workspace are supported today. Outlook / Microsoft 365 support is coming soon and will be available on all paid plans.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes — switching to yearly billing saves you 17% (two months free) on both Standard and Pro. Toggle the billing switch at the top of this page to see yearly prices.
